OpenWalkieTalkie 0.6.0 adds Arcade: three offline games on the deck's full 480×480 display, with original synthesized 8-bit music and sound effects you can turn off.

## What changed

- Add **Flappy Bird**: press PTT to flap. PTT is a local game button on Arcade and does not open the microphone or send an agent message.
- Add **Snake**: turn with the wheel, with Left and Right as backup controls. The board fills the display, and corner walls keep the snake and food clear of the panel's rounded corners.
- Add **Raiden (雷电)**: move with Left and Right while the plane fires automatically. Enemy aircraft face downward toward the player; three lives are shown beside the score.
- Use full-screen playfields, enlarged pixel art, and overlaid scores. Control hints appear on the ready and pause screens. Enter starts, pauses, resumes, or retries; Cancel returns to the game list. Switching away pauses play.
- Add **Arcade → Sound** with **Off**, **SFX only**, and **Music + SFX**. Off is the default. Three original music loops and seven effects are synthesized on the device; volume follows Speaker. Local game audio yields to host speech and capture.
- Teach the Mac service the Arcade face and sound setting. Existing saved face layouts are preserved. With the matching service connected, sound preferences are saved and restored; without it, the setting lasts for the current boot. Best scores last until restart.

## Install and play

Install the signed, notarized macOS app for Apple Silicon or Intel, or use **Check for Updates**. macOS 13 or later is required. Confirm that the app and service both report **0.6.0**, connect one deck over USB-C, then use **Service → Updates → Check firmware**.

For an existing saved layout, **double-tap Shift → + → Arcade → Enter**, then double-tap Shift to leave the face manager. Arcade is already included in the default layout for new installations. Select a game and press Enter to start. Use **Arcade → Sound → Enter** to choose the audio mode.

Devices already on the 0.4.0/0.5.0 partition layout and recovery protocol 2 receive a normal application update. **Devices still on the old bootloader require guided migration. Keep USB-C power connected and the Mac awake: losing power during bootloader replacement can prevent startup and USB recovery and require hardware repair.** Accept the app's warning before migration. Do not manually install bootstrap or recovery files as application firmware.

## Validation and downloads

Game simulation, sound synthesis, protocol, saved settings, and retained-frame rendering have software coverage. Rev A development builds were flashed and tested during development, including full-screen play and the corrected enemy orientation. Rev B gameplay and audible music/effects still need physical testing; no device audio was played during this work.

The release includes both Mac packages, signed update feeds, Rev A/B application and migration images, ELFs, factory images, updater manifests, SHA-256 checksums, build attestations, and a Chinese upgrade guide (`README.zh-CN.md`, also inside the DMGs).

**The factory ZIP is for blank-board provisioning and overwrites all flash contents, including settings and pairing data. Existing devices should use the GUI updater.**
