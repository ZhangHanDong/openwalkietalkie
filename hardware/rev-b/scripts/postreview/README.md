# Post-review edits (2026-10-05/06)

An independent review of the first release candidate (mechanical, RF, audio,
electrical, DFM, completeness) was followed by these board edits. They were
made with KiCad python one-offs. This folder records them as scripts, in order.
Step 12 is a later change requested by the user, not a review finding. The
released board file is the authority.

1. `fix_rules.txt`: the `.kicad_dru` was regenerated by `../board_rules.py`:
   - "RF50 at the U5 pins" now excludes zones, so the GND pours keep the
     0.2 mm CPWG gap (it was 0.10 mm board-wide).
   - "audio summing nodes" moved after "audio analog spacing" (the last
     matching rule wins) and now applies to any non-audio, non-GND copper.
2. `reroute_d1_d3.sh`: PANEL_D1/PANEL_D3 were moved off the antenna ground
   edge (0.04 mm → 0.31 / 0.42 mm from the clearance). `../maze.py` was run with
   a keep-out halo around the antenna clearance, after deleting their copper
   and restoring the east-face and U3 escape vias.
3. `c22_cell.py`: flash decoupler C22 now connects straight to U4 pin 8 on B
   (1 mm; it was 11.5 mm through the AVDD33_AUD star node, running beside
   BUCK_LX). The CELL sense line was re-routed between its pads, under the 0402 body.
4. `../dedupe_tracks.py`: removed 49 exact duplicate segments left by
   multi-island maze joins.
5. `../stitch_extra.py`, 30 GND vias in total:
   - along the antenna clearance edge
   - in the gaps of the CPWG fence
   - beside each RF shunt pad (shunt GND pads set to solid connection)
   - beside the DAC pair

   It also adds In1/In2 fill voids (pad + 0.2 mm) under the RF network pads.
6. `dfm.py`:
   - no paste on DNP pads (C52, C53, C58, L6, R44)
   - U5 pin-1 dot moved 0.05 mm away from the mask opening
   - TP*/MH* excluded from position files and BOM
   - title block revision B
   - U5 VCC link to C28 widened to 0.2 mm where it fits
   - "OpenWalkieTalkie main Rev B / 2026-10" silkscreen label
7. PANEL_CLK was re-routed with `../maze.py --reroute`, with a B-side keep-out
   around Y1 (u 39.9–43.2, v 29.5–32.6). This moved its via from 0.14 mm to
   0.575 mm from the crystal pads, but left a 3.69 mm In2 run under the
   XO/XI escapes (fixed in step 9).
8. `../dedupe_tracks.py` (now also drops segments lying inside a longer
   collinear one), then `cell_stub.py`, which deletes a redundant CELL path
   left by step 3.
9. `clk_reroute.sh`: PANEL_CLK and PANEL_D2 re-routed by `../maze.py --ripup`
   with In2 barred under Y1. Some panel/CAL/AMP_ENABLE segments were ripped
   up and re-routed along the way. PANEL_CLK is now F/B only, and the nearest
   signal via is 0.78 mm from a Y1 pad, edge to edge.
10. `../r39_via.py`: R39.2's GND via at (33.22, 30.0) stays (without it R39.2
    and a B pour piece were cut off). An In2 no-fill rule area keeps In2 from
    leaving a 0.1 mm² sliver between USB_MCU_P/N there.
11. `../dedupe_tracks.py` once more (3 duplicates left by `clean_loop.sh`'s
    end bridging in step 9), then `../finalize.sh BOARD REL --no-pour`, then
    `../final_checks.py` for `verification/final-checks.txt`.
13. 2026-10-06b: `round_br.py` rounds the bottom-right board corner to match the
    top-left (replaces its Edge.Cuts chain with a 180-degree-rotated copy of the
    top-left corner chain about (28.0, 47.5); the bottom edge end shifts from
    u 47.50 to 47.99 to meet it). `fit_check.py` confirms it only removes board
    material and does not cover any case feature; `case_br.py` (in the build
    session) sectioned BodyV6.13 to check fit. Then `../finalize.sh --no-pour`,
    the 3D fit re-run, and `../netlist_diff.py` / `../final_checks.py` (both
    unchanged).
12. 2026-10-06: `../r4r5_22k.py` sets R4/R5 back to r6's 22k (RC0402FR-0722KL,
    C25768). They stay at the NS4150B pins. `../build_bom.py` lost its R4/R5
    override, so their part data (C25768, RC0402FR-0722KL) comes from r6's
    BOM again. Then
    `../finalize.sh BOARD REL --no-pour`, `../netlist_diff.py` for
    `verification/netlist-diff-vs-r6.txt`, and the 3D fit (populated STEP in
    the case frame, fit assembly, fit report) was re-run on the new export.
