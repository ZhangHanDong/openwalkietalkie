# Independent review of the Rev B release candidate (2026-10-05)

Six reviewers checked the first release candidate (mechanical/3D, RF,
audio, electrical, DFM, completeness) by measuring the board with pcbnew,
kicad-cli and the case STEP. Every blocker/major finding then went to a
separate verifier who tried to refute it. The table lists each finding that
needed action and what the released board does about it.

| ID | Severity (verified) | Finding | Disposition in the release |

Apart from the RF feed, GND and the DAC pair (RF-8), the nearest other copper is PANEL_RESET at R30, 2.38 mm; everything else is ≥ 3.1 mm. The panel bus could not be fitted west of the DAC pair with the other lines in place. Listed in README "Known limitations" with a BLE RSSI/PER test for the first build. |

Change after the review (2026-10-06b): the board's **bottom-right corner was
rounded to match the top-left** at the user's request. The sharp diagonal
cut-ins were replaced with the top-left corner's smooth tangent fillets (a
180-degree-rotated copy about the board centre), keeping the 2.0 mm case
locating lobe. It only removes copper-free board material (~3 mm2, verified by
a board-polygon set difference: added 0.002 mm2); no part, net or track moved
(BOM/CPL/netlist unchanged), DRC is 0/0, the audio check is 0 errors, and the
3D fit against BodyV6.13 shows no new interference and no board-case
intersection. A case section at the board plane confirmed the fit and also that
the case's bottom-right pocket wall is not symmetric with the top-left, so the
board now sits with a small gap (up to ~2 mm) at that corner; matching the case
cosmetically is a separate Fusion edit, not done here.

Change after the review (2026-10-06): R4/R5 went back from 100k to r6's
22k, because R_in is not what caused the r6 hiss. They stay at the NS4150B
input pins. Only the BOM line, the position-file values and the 3D models
changed; copper, mask, silk and paste are identical. DRC 0/0 and the audio
check (0 errors) were re-run.

What the reviewers confirmed as correct:
- All seven requests implemented as stated, with the netlist diff matching the intent exactly.
- J3 pinout and footprint (KiCad stock 53261-0371 geometry).
- Switch centring.
- 3D fit and mounting holes as r6, and B-side heights. (The bottom-right corner was later rounded to match the top-left; see the change note below.)
- Power-path resistances, all lower than r6.
- No plane islands.
- Fab outputs complete and matching the board, with JLC 4-layer limits met with margin.
