Cookie information

Updated October 16, 2026

The website currently includes no advertising or analytics scripts. You can browse and join the waitlist without accepting optional tracking.

What your browser remembers

  • Day/night theme: owt-theme in local storage remembers the theme you select. It stays on this browser until you clear it. If no choice is saved, we use your operating system's preference.
  • Privacy notice: selecting “Got it” saves owt-privacy-notice in local storage so the notice stays dismissed for 180 days. It contains a notice version and expiry time, not an identifier. Acknowledging it does not consent to tracking.

These preferences are not sent to the waitlist API. Local storage is similar to cookies in that it saves information on your device, but it is not automatically attached to requests.

Cloudflare security cookies

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website and may set cookies when security features or challenges are used. Which cookies appear and how long they last depend on those features. Read Cloudflare's cookie descriptions for details.

Manage browser storage

You can remove this site's cookies and local storage using your browser's site-data settings. Clearing storage resets the theme and shows the privacy notice again. Blocking security cookies may prevent a Cloudflare security challenge from completing.

Show the privacy notice again. If JavaScript is disabled, this page still explains all the storage used by the site.

Your waitlist email

Your email is stored securely on the server when you submit the waitlist form. Clearing browser storage does not remove a signup. To leave the list, email privacy@cohdl.ai. See the Privacy Policy for collection, retention, and other requests.
